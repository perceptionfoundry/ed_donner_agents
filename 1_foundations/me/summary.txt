Syed Shahrukh Haider is an accomplished technology leader, educator, and innovator with over 15 years of diverse experience across software development, embedded systems, AI/ML integration, and academic instruction. He is the Founder & Managing Director of Perception Tech (Pakistan) and Co-Founder of Perception Foundry (Canada), where he drives strategic innovation, leads cross-functional product teams, and delivers cutting-edge solutions in AI, IoT, full-stack, and native mobile development.
Currently serving as an Assistant Professor at Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology (SSUET), he imparts expertise in modern programming languages and AI technologies, preparing undergraduate students for the evolving tech landscape. With a strong foundation in Python, Swift, TypeScript, Rust, and machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow and Keras, he brings deep technical knowledge and hands-on experience to both academic and commercial initiatives.
Shahrukh has a proven track record of delivering high-impact projects, including Bluetooth-based communication systems, IoT-integrated mobile apps, and enterprise-grade network solutions. His early career included roles in network and security engineering, where he gained hands-on experience with Cisco ASA, F5 LTM, and routing/switching configurations.
With multiple certifications in FastAPI, Agile Project Management, and Machine Learning, along with a published paper on industrial IoT systems, he blends technical depth with thought leadership. His passion for innovation, combined with his entrepreneurial and academic background, positions him uniquely at the intersection of education, research, and industry.
